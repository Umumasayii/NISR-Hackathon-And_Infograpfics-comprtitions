**Data sources.** National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR): Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) annual tables 2019–2025 as published, SAS survey microdata 2019–2025 (Seasons A, B and C), and Agricultural Household Survey (AHS) microdata 2017, 2020 and 2024. Rainfall: CHIRPS v2, 1981–2025. District boundaries: GADM 4.1. Statistics cover 2019–2025 only.

**Which figure is shown.** Where NISR publishes a figure that answers the same question with the same definition (for example the share of farmers who irrigated, fertiliser use, cultivated land, crop production, harvested area and yield), the app shows NISR's figure, cited to its table and cell, and keeps our own estimate in the Methods register as a cross-check. Where NISR publishes a figure with a different definition, it is shown under its own label. Where NISR publishes no figure (for example the share of plots irrigated, irrigated against rain-fed yields, the screening shortlist), the figure is our own survey-weighted estimate from the microdata, labelled as such. NISR publishes national and district figures: for several districts or a province the app says "Published breakdown unavailable".

**Independence.** This is an independent project: it is not affiliated with or endorsed by NISR.
