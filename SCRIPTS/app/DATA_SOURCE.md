**Data sources.** National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) survey microdata: Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) 2019–2025, Seasons A, B and C, and Agricultural Household Survey (AHS) 2017, 2020 and 2024. Rainfall: CHIRPS v2, 1981–2025. District boundaries: GADM 4.1. Statistics cover 2019–2025 only.

**What the numbers are.** Every figure is our own survey-weighted estimate from the microdata (or a model built on it), not an official NISR statistic. Published NISR figures are kept in a separate register with their table, sheet and cell, and are never written over our estimates. This is an independent project: it is not affiliated with or endorsed by NISR.
