**Data sources.** National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR): Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) 2019–2025 microdata, the SAS 2024 Annual Report, the SAS 2025 Season A report and the published SAS 2026 Season A and Season B tables; Agricultural Household Survey (AHS) 2017, 2020 and 2024 microdata. Rainfall: CHIRPS v2, 1981–2025. District boundaries: GADM 4.1. NISR published figures are shown as published and labelled "NISR published"; project estimates from the survey microdata are shown beside them, never in their place. Each chart names its own source.

This is an independent team project for the NISR 2026 Big Data Hackathon. It is not an official NISR product, and NISR does not endorse its suggestions.
