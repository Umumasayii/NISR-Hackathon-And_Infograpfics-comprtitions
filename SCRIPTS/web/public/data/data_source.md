**Data sources.** National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR): Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) 2019–2025 microdata and the published SAS 2026 Season A and Season B reports; Agricultural Household Survey (AHS) 2017, 2020 and 2024. Rainfall: CHIRPS v2, 1981–2025. District boundaries: GADM 4.1. Where an indicator is published by NISR, the app shows the published figure.
